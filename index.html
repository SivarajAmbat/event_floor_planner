Planning assumptions

This tool is intended for preliminary event-space planning. Capacity estimates are based on geometric table, chair and clearance assumptions and should be validated against the venue's actual architectural floor plan, fire/life-safety requirements, accessibility requirements and venue rules.

  • 1 chair = 1 participant.
  • Chairs are modelled as 1.4 × 1.4 ft, set 0.1 ft from the table edge.
  • Each table plus its chairs forms a group; groups must stay at least the walking clearance apart and the wall clearance from the walls.
  • "Very tight" means a smallest aisle under 3 ft, or groups plus aisles filling 90%+ of the usable floor.
